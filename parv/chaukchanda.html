PARV 14 OF 30 · BHADO

Chaukchanda (Chauth Chandra)

चौरचन · चौठचंद

Bhado Shukla Chaturthi (August–September)

"चौरचन के चान, हाथ में दही-केरा"

Katha · The story

Why we keep it

Chaukchanda is Mithila's own festival and it rests on a curse. Ganesh, riding his mouse one night, fell, and the moon laughed at him. Ganesh cursed the moon: whoever sees you on this night, the fourth of Bhado, will be falsely accused of theft. The gods begged him to soften it, and he did: whoever looks at the moon with an offering in hand, having worshipped Ganesh, will be spared. Krishna himself, the story says, once saw the moon bare-handed on this night and was accused of stealing the Syamantak jewel, and cleared his name only after a long search.

So on Chaukchanda evening, every Maithil, from the oldest to the youngest, stands in the courtyard holding a fruit, a sweet, a banana leaf with dahi on it, and looks up at the fourth-day moon through the offering before eating it.

Mahatva · Significance

What it means

It is the Maithil form of Ganesh Chaturthi, kept in the courtyard and not the pandal. It honours Ganesh as the remover of obstacles and the moon as the keeper of the calendar. Its lesson, that a false accusation is the worst fate and that an empty hand invites it, is told to every child.

"चौठ के चनरमा, दही-चूड़ा हाथ"
A line everyone at the festival knows

Vidhi · How it is done in Malinagar

Step by step

  1. The courtyard is lipa with cow dung and an aripan drawn. Ganesh and a kalash are established at dusk.
  2. Kheer, dahi, kela, kasar and the season's fruit are arranged on banana leaves.
  3. When the moon shows, the eldest offers arghya; then each person looks at the moon with an offering in both hands and the katha is told.
  4. The offerings are eaten, and the evening ends with the moon song.

Bhojan · What is eaten

Kheer, dahi, banana, kasar, pedakiya; in many houses the first makhana of the year.

Yaad rakhiye · Good to know

Children in the city are reminded by a morning phone call: tonight, don't look at the moon with an empty hand.
