PARV 21 OF 30 · KARTIK

Diwali: dibiya aur diya

दिवाली · दीपावली

Kartik Amavasya (October–November)

"पहिल दीया सोखा बाबा के"

Katha · The story

Why we keep it

Ram returned to Ayodhya on the new-moon night of Kartik after fourteen years of exile and the war at Lanka, and the people lit rows of lamps so that he could find his way in the dark. That is the Diwali of the Ramayan. The Diwali of the village is older than that: it is the night the house, the fields, the cattle shed, the well and the gachhi are shown to Lakshmi, one lamp at a time, so that she knows where the family lives.

The blog's post "Bachpan ki Diwali" remembers how it went in Malinagar. Houses whitewashed a week before. Crackers bought days early and dried in the sun so they would not fizzle. Two kinds of lamp: the dibiya, a small clay kerosene lamp with a lid, and the diya with mustard oil or ghee. The first diya at Sokha Baba, the household deity, then at Kali Badi, then home, the fields, the cattle shed, the well and the orchard. Children ran about with ukka, bundles of dried sticks and grass set alight and whirled. Dinner was rice, dal, pakoras, bhajji and sweets, and the sweets were the point.

Mahatva · Significance

What it means

Diwali is Lakshmi's night and Ram's homecoming, but in Malinagar it is first the night the whole of a family's world is lit: the deity, the fields, the animals, the water and the trees. The order of the lamps is the village's map of what matters.

"दीया जरे सोखा बाबा दुआरी, फेर जरे काली बाड़ी"
A line everyone at the festival knows

Vidhi · How it is done in Malinagar

Step by step

  1. Dhanteras, two days before: the house cleaned, a new vessel bought, the first diya lit outside for Yama.
  2. Diwali morning: the last whitewash, rangoli at the door, the dibiyas filled.
  3. Dusk: Lakshmi-Ganesh puja at home. Then the lamp procession: Sokha Baba first, Kali Badi, the house, the fields, the gowshala, the well, the gachhi.
  4. Night: crackers and ukka for the children, sweets, and the lamps kept burning till the oil runs out.

Bhojan · What is eaten

Pakoras and bhajji, kheer, pedakiya, laddoo, and the mithai from the chowk.

Yaad rakhiye · Good to know

The old dibiya with its lid is rarer every year. If your house still has one, light it this Diwali and send a photo for the gallery.
