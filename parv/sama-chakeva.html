PARV 24 OF 30 · KARTIK

Sama Chakeva

सामा चकेवा

Kartik Shukla Saptami (after Chhath) to Kartik Purnima (November)

"सामा खेले चललीं, भैया के बगिया"

Katha · The story

Why we keep it

Sama was Krishna's daughter, and she loved to wander in the forests with the rishis. A courtier, Chugla the backbiter, told Krishna she was meeting men there, and Krishna in anger cursed her to become a bird. Her brother Chakeva, who loved her, did penance until Krishna relented, and Sama was returned to human form. Chugla's lies were exposed and his moustache set on fire. The sisters of Mithila have sung this story every Kartik for centuries, and it is also the story of the birds that come to Mithila's ponds in winter.

For eight evenings after Chhath, the girls of each tola shape Sama, Chakeva, Chugla, the birds, the dog and the sati-bihula from clay, paint them, keep them in a bamboo basket and take them out at dusk to sing. On Kartik Purnima night the brothers come to the jowar fields, the sisters give them the clay figures, Chugla's moustache is burnt, and Sama is seen off with the last song until next year.

Mahatva · Significance

What it means

It is the Maithil festival of brother and sister, a counterpart to Rakhi, and of the migratory birds whose arrival marks winter. Its figures and songs are among the oldest folk art of Mithila. It also keeps the girls of a tola together in the week after Chhath, when the men have mostly left again.

"सामा चकेवा अइहा हे, ई साल के जइहा हे"
A line everyone at the festival knows

Vidhi · How it is done in Malinagar

Step by step

  1. The day after Chhath, clay is dug from the pokhar and the figures made: Sama, Chakeva, Chugla, sati-bihula, the birds, the dog, the basket-bearer.
  2. Every evening the girls sit in a circle with the basket of figures, the diya lit, and sing the Sama geet.
  3. Kartik Purnima night: procession to the fields with the basket, the brothers receive the figures, Chugla's moustache is burnt with a wick.
  4. Sama is seen off with the song "Sama chakeva aiha he, ai saal ke jaiha he", and the figures are left in the field or the pond.

Bhojan · What is eaten

Chura, dahi, the makhana and gur the brothers bring, and the sweets exchanged on Purnima.

Yaad rakhiye · Good to know

Sama Chakeva is fading in the cities and alive in villages like ours. If the girls of your tola still make the figures, the gallery wants a photo.
